    Privacy Policy

    Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Viral Son collects, how cookies and Google AdSense operate on our site, and the choices you control.

    Last Updated: November 24, 2026

    Our Privacy Commitment

    Viral Son respects your personal data. We collect only the minimum information required to operate our independent news, technology, and music publication, answer reader messages, deliver optional newsletters, and fund free journalism through privacy-conscious advertising partners such as Google AdSense.

    This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, readers, and contributors interacting with Viral Son ("we", "us", "our"). By using our website, you agree to the practices described below and in our Cookie Policy and Terms & Conditions.

    1 Who We Are & Data Controller

    Viral Son is an independent digital publication managed by Michael Edet (Founder & Editor-in-Chief). For all privacy inquiries, data access requests, or opt-out assistance, our privacy contact can be reached directly at iamoffixial@gmail.com.

    2 Information We Collect

    We collect two categories of information when you interact with Viral Son:

    2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

    • Contact & Advertising Forms: Name, email address, optional phone number, inquiry category, and message content when you contact us or request advertising packages.
    • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address when opting into our daily or weekly editorial dispatches.
    • Reader Comments: Display name, email address (never published publicly), and comment text when joining article discussions.

    2.2 Automatically Collected Log & Device Data

    • Standard web server log data such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/exit URLs, viewport dimensions, and timestamps used for security, spam defense, and site reliability.
    • Aggregated audience measurement and ad-delivery signals handled via cookies and similar browser technologies.

    3 Cookies & Browser Storage

    Viral Son uses first-party browser storage and third-party cookies to preserve your reading experience and support free access to articles:

    • Theme Preference (viralson_theme): Remembers whether you prefer Light or Dark Mode.
    • Consent & Modal State (viralson_cookie_consent, viralson_newsletter_suppressed): Remembers your cookie preferences and prevents repetitive popups.
    • Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Used by Google and its certified ad partners to serve relevant ads, limit ad repetition, and detect invalid click fraud.

    You can inspect every cookie we use and adjust your consent settings anytime on our dedicated Cookie Policy page.

    4 Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

    Viral Son uses Google AdSense to display advertisements that keep our reporting, tech guides, and music previews free for readers.

    Important Google AdSense Disclosure: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to Viral Son or other websites on the internet. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our site and/or other sites across the web.

    How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

    • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
    • Alternatively, visitors in the United States and globally can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Tool.
    • For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, non-personalized ads or consent-gated ad delivery are respected according to regional law.

    5 How We Use Your Information

    • To respond to story tips, corrections, DMCA notices, technical issues, and sponsorship inquiries;
    • To deliver newsletters you explicitly requested (every email includes one-click unsubscribe);
    • To moderate comments, defend against automated spam/abuse, and maintain server security;
    • To remember your Dark Mode and accessibility settings; and
    • To measure aggregate readership trends and sustain the publication via compliant advertising.

    We never sell your personal contact details, email address, or private messages to data brokers.

    6 Third-Party Links & Embedded Media

    Articles may contain outbound reference links or embedded audio/media previews from external platforms. Once you leave Viral Son or interact with an external embed, that third party’s own privacy policy governs any data it collects. Please review their policies before sharing personal data.

    7 EEA & UK Privacy Rights (GDPR)

    If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the right under the GDPR/UK GDPR to:

    • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
    • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal details;
    • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your contact records or comments;
    • Withdraw Consent / Object: Opt out of newsletters or non-essential cookies at any time; and
    • Lodge a Complaint: Contact your local Data Protection Authority if you believe your rights were infringed.

    8 California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

    California residents have the right to know what categories of personal information we collect, request deletion of personal information collected directly from them, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise these rights, manage cookie preferences on our Cookie Policy or email iamoffixial@gmail.com.

    9 Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

    Viral Son is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal data through a form or comment, please contact iamoffixial@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

    10 Data Retention & Contact Us

    We retain contact inquiry records only as long as necessary to resolve your request and maintain legal records. You may request deletion or update of any record at any time.
