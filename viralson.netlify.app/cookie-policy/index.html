    1 What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

    Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. Modern browsers also provide localStorage and sessionStorage to remember user interface preferences (such as Light/Dark theme) locally on your browser without sending unnecessary tracking requests.

    2 Detailed Breakdown of Cookies & Storage Used

    Name / Key Provider Category Purpose Retention
    viralson_theme Viral Son (First-Party) Essential / Preference Stores your Light or Dark Mode selection to prevent screen flicker. Persistent (until cleared)
    viralson_cookie_consent Viral Son (First-Party) Essential Records your cookie consent choices made on this page. 12 months
    viralson_newsletter_suppressed Viral Son (First-Party) Preference Remembers if you dismissed or subscribed to the newsletter modal so we don’t re-prompt you. 14–60 days
    viralson_anchor_dismissed Viral Son (First-Party) Preference Remembers if you closed the bottom banner during your current browsing session. Session

    3 Third-Party & Google Advertising Cookies

    Google, as a third-party vendor on Viral Son, uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet. Visitors in the EEA and UK may choose whether to allow personalized advertising cookies or receive non-personalized contextual ads.

    • Opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings.
    • Manage industry-wide advertising opt-outs via YourOnlineChoices (EU) or AboutAds (US).

    5 Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

    You can also block or delete cookies directly through your browser settings:

    • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
    • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
    • Apple Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
    • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
