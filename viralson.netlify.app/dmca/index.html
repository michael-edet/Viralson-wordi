    1 Respect for Copyright

    Viral Son responds expeditiously to notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA", 17 U.S.C. § 512) and equivalent international copyright frameworks. Upon receipt of a complete, valid notice, we will promptly remove or disable access to the identified material and notify the relevant contributor or rights coordinator.

    2 How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

    To be legally effective under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your written notification must include all of the following six elements:

    1. Signature: A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner’s behalf.
    2. Identification of Copyrighted Work: Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., original publication URL, registration number, or track ISRC).
    3. Infringing Material URL: The exact URL(s) on Viral Son where the allegedly infringing material is located so our team can locate and review it quickly.
    4. Your Contact Details: Your full legal name, organization (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and active email address.
    5. Good-Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law (including fair use).
    6. Accuracy & Authority Statement: A statement that the information in your notification is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

    Please note that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be subject to liability for damages.

    3 Promotional Music & Fair Use Journalism

    Many music previews and editorial screenshots on Viral Son are published either under artist/label promotional submission or under news reporting, review, and commentary principles. If an artist, label, or photographer would like updated attribution links, streaming embeds, or immediate removal of a promotional file, emailing iamoffixial@gmail.com resolves most requests immediately.

    4 Counter-Notification Procedure

    If content you submitted was removed due to a mistake or misidentification, you may send a written counter-notice containing:

    • Your physical or electronic signature;
    • Identification of the removed material and its prior URL;
    • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification; and
    • Your name, address, phone number, and consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate federal court for resolution.

    5 Repeat Infringer Policy

    In appropriate circumstances, Viral Son will permanently terminate the accounts or contributor privileges of users or guest authors who repeatedly infringe third-party copyrights.
